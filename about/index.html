The story behind Tommy Stories

Tommy Stories started at bedtime.

When we were little, my dad told us bedtime stories he called Tommy Stories. They touched every part of life, and somehow they always seemed to touch cars.

Growing up in Des Moines

My dad grew up in Des Moines, Iowa, and worked for his dad, who was a house painter. Those painting jobs gave him some of his best stories.

There was riding to jobs in the back of a Ford van, sitting on a bucket with no seatbelt. And there was the trip he spent riding up in the over-cab camper on a 1974 Ford F-100, when he got sick and had to signal to his parents through the sliding window to let them know what was going on.

The lime green Camaro

Then there was his 1976 Camaro Type LT, in lime green. He put air shocks on it, and later he sold it.

Many years later, after more than 15 years of looking, we found that exact car.

My brother's Camaro

Some of the stories that have touched me are our own. My brother's first car, in high school, was a 2000 Camaro SS. Hard times forced us to give it up.

Years later, I stumbled on that same car for sale on eBay Motors. By then our circumstances had changed, and we were able to make an offer and buy it back.

Why Tommy Stories

Every one of those stories has a car in it, but they're really about family, work, hard times and growing up. Everyone has stories like that.

Tommy Stories is a place to share them. Every month, we mail four true stories from readers, in large print, each with a car somewhere in it.
