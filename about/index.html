The story behind Tommy Stories

Tommy Stories started at bedtime.

When we were little, bedtime meant a Tommy Story. That's what my dad called the stories he told us at night. They wandered through every part of life, and somehow they always found their way to a car.

Growing up in Des Moines

My dad grew up in Des Moines, Iowa, working for his dad, a house painter. Some of the best Tommy Stories came from those painting jobs.

He rode to them in the back of a Ford van, perched on a bucket, with no seatbelt in sight.

Then there was the trip in the over-cab camper on a 1974 Ford F-100. He was riding up above the cab when he got sick, and the only way to tell his parents was through the sliding window between the camper and the cab. If you've ever tried to explain that through a little window, you know it isn't easy.

The lime green Camaro

And then there was his 1976 Camaro Type LT, in lime green, with the air shocks he put in himself. Eventually, he sold it.

We never forgot that car. After more than 15 years of looking, we found it again: the very same Camaro.

My brother's Camaro

Some of the stories that have touched me are our own. My brother's first car, back in high school, was a 2000 Camaro SS. Then hard times came, and we had to let it go.

Years later, I stumbled on that same car for sale on eBay Motors. By then our circumstances had changed, and we were able to make an offer and bring it back into the family.

Why Tommy Stories

Cars have a way of carrying us through the biggest moments of life. They take us to work and home again, to weddings and hospitals, to church on Sunday and back to the people we love. Every one of these stories has a car in it, but each one is really about life, family and faith.

We started Tommy Stories so we could all share stories like these openly, and so that reading them might inspire others to grow closer to their faith. Every month, we mail four true stories from readers, in large print, each with a car somewhere in it.
