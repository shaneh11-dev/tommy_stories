What's inside each issue

Every issue is a booklet made for a comfortable afternoon of reading, and every envelope holds a little surprise from Tommy.

In every issue

  • A Note from Tommy

    A short letter to open the issue.

  • Four True Car Stories

    About life, family and faith, each one written by the reader who lived it.

  • Do You Remember?

    A then-and-now look at one year: what a new car, a gallon of gas or a movie ticket cost.

  • Name That Car

    Guess the classic car from a few clues. The fewer clues you need, the more points you score.

  • The Puzzle Page

    A car word search or crossword in large print, with the answers in the back.

  • Letters to Tommy

    Readers write back about the stories, the cars and the memories they stirred up.

  • A Surprise from Tommy

    Tucked in every envelope: something small and real you can hold, handpicked by Tommy and different every month, like a card from a car trivia board game.

  • Large, high-contrast print
  • Short reads of 5 to 10 minutes, good for reading aloud

Dear Tommy Stories,

Have a story worth telling?

Tommy Stories is written by readers, and your story doesn't have to be about a car. Write about your life, your family or your faith. It only needs a car somewhere in it, like the one you drove to the hospital, the one parked out front on your wedding day, or the one you learned to drive in.

Share your story

*Because we receive so many stories, we can't publish every one, and a story may run several months after it's sent.

What we look for

  • A true story about your life, your family or your faith
  • A car somewhere in it, even a small part
  • About 600 to 1,200 words
  • Typed or handwritten, sent online, by email or by mail

Common questions

When will my first issue arrive?

We mail each issue toward the end of the month. Subscribe by the 10th and your first issue is that month's. After the 10th, it's next month's.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, online under Manage my subscription or by email. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. You'll still receive the issues you've already paid for.

Can I send it to someone else?

Yes. Subscribe monthly or yearly and enter their name and address for shipping at checkout. It keeps coming to them every month until you cancel. Or give a gift of 6 months, 1 year or 2 years, paid once.
