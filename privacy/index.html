Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Tommy Stories is run by Tommy Stories LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company (“we” or “us”). We collect only what we need to mail your issues, look after your subscription and understand how our website is used. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • When you subscribe or give a gift: name, mailing address, email, and phone if you give it. Card details go directly to Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your card number.
  • When you send a story or a letter: your name, contact details, what you wrote, any photos, and how you'd like your name printed.
  • When you invite friends: your name and email, each friend's email, and your note.
  • When you contact us: whatever you include in your email or letter.
  • When you visit our website: which pages you visit, the kind of device and browser you use, your general area (such as your city or state), and the website or link that brought you here. See Website visits below.

How we use it

  • To mail your issues and handle billing, cancellations and address changes.
  • To answer your questions and contact you about stories you send.
  • To send one email confirming we received your story or letter, if you give us your email address.
  • To tell you about important changes to your subscription, such as a price change.
  • To see which pages people read and how they find us, so we can make our website easier to use.
  • When you invite friends: to send each one invitation with your note, one reminder a week later, and you a copy. We never add your friends, or you, to a mailing list. After the reminder, we delete the invitation, including your friend's email address. Anyone can stop our emails with the link at the bottom of each one; for them we keep only a scrambled fingerprint of their address, so we never email them again.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run Tommy Stories: our payment processor (Stripe), our printing and mailing partner (your name and mailing address, so they can mail your issue), and the services that host our website and email. Each one gets only what it needs, through its own secure systems, and may use your information only to provide its service to us. We also use Google Analytics to count visits to our website (see Website visits).

Website visits

We use Google Analytics to count visits to our website: how many people come, which pages they read and how they found us. It uses cookies (small files your browser keeps) to tell one visit from another. It doesn't receive your name, email, mailing address or anything you type into our forms, and we don't use it to show you ads. Google handles this information as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. To turn it off, block cookies in your browser's settings or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

When we print your story or letter

If we print a story or letter you send, your name appears the way you chose: first name and last initial, first name only, or initials, each with your city and state, or no name at all. A printed story may also appear on our website and in future Tommy Stories collections, as our terms explain. We never print your street address, email or phone number.

How we protect it

  • Card numbers go straight to Stripe, a certified payment processor. We never see or store them.
  • Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Only we can sign in to the accounts that hold your information, and they're protected with strong passwords and two-step login.
  • We keep your information while your subscription is active, and afterwards only as long as we need it for tax and accounting records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email support@tommystories.com.
