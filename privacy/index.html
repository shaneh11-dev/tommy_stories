Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

We collect only what we need to mail your issues and look after your subscription. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • When you subscribe or give a gift: name, mailing address, email, and phone if you give it. Card details go directly to Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your card number.
  • When you send a story: your name, contact details, your story, and how you'd like your name printed.
  • When you contact us: whatever you include in your email or letter.

How we use it

  • To mail your issues and handle billing, cancellations and address changes.
  • To answer your questions and contact you about stories you send.
  • To tell you about important changes to your subscription, such as a price change.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run Tommy Stories: our payment processor (Stripe), our printing and mailing partner (your name and mailing address, so they can mail your issue), and the services that host our website and email. They may use your information only to provide those services.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email support@tommystories.com.
