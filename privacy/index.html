Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Tommy Stories is run by Tommy Stories LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company (“we” or “us”). We collect only what we need to mail your issues and look after your subscription. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • When you subscribe or give a gift: name, mailing address, email, and phone if you give it. Card details go directly to Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your card number.
  • When you send a story or a letter: your name, contact details, what you wrote, any photos, and how you'd like your name printed.
  • When you contact us: whatever you include in your email or letter.

How we use it

  • To mail your issues and handle billing, cancellations and address changes.
  • To answer your questions and contact you about stories you send.
  • To send one email confirming we received your story or letter, if you give us your email address.
  • To tell you about important changes to your subscription, such as a price change.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run Tommy Stories: our payment processor (Stripe), our printing and mailing partner (your name and mailing address, so they can mail your issue), and the services that host our website and email. Each one gets only what it needs, through its own secure systems, and may use your information only to provide its service to us.

When we print your story or letter

If we print a story or letter you send, your name appears the way you chose: first name and last initial, first name only, or initials, each with your city and state, or no name at all. A printed story may also appear on our website and in future Tommy Stories collections, as our terms explain. We never print your street address, email or phone number.

How we protect it

  • Card numbers go straight to Stripe, a certified payment processor. We never see or store them.
  • Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Only we can sign in to the accounts that hold your information, and they're protected with strong passwords and two-step login.
  • We keep your information while your subscription is active, and afterwards only as long as we need it for tax and accounting records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email support@tommystories.com.
