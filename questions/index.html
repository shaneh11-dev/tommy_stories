Questions

Can't find your answer here? Email support@tommystories.com or call [PHONE]. We reply within one business day.

How much does it cost?

$16.99 a month, with shipping to any U.S. address included. You're billed each month on the date you signed up.

When will my first issue arrive?

We mail each issue around the middle of the month. Subscribe by the 10th and your first issue is that month's; after the 10th, it's next month's. Issues usually arrive within one to two weeks of mailing.

What's in each issue?

Four true stories written by readers, about cars, family, life and faith. Each one is edited lightly for length and clarity and printed in large type.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage my subscription and sign in with the email you used at checkout. You can also email or call us and we'll cancel it for you. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.

How do I change my mailing address?

Update it under Manage my subscription, or email or call us. Changes made by the 10th apply to that month's issue.

Can I pay by check?

Yes. Mail a check for 6 months ($101.94) or 12 months ($203.88), made out to Tommy Stories, with your name and mailing address to:

Tommy Stories
[MAILING ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Gift subscriptions come in 3, 6 or 12 months, paid once, and don't renew on their own.

My issue didn't arrive. What should I do?

Email or call us and we'll mail a replacement.

Do you mail outside the United States?

Not yet. We mail to U.S. addresses only for now.

How do I share my own story?

Send it through our story form or by mail. We look for true stories of about 600 to 1,200 words.
