Questions

Can't find your answer here? Email support@tommystories.com. We reply within one business day.

How much does it cost?

$16.99 a month or $199.99 a year, with shipping to any U.S. address included. Monthly plans are billed each month, and yearly plans once a year, on the date you signed up.

Does the yearly plan renew?

Yes, once a year on the date you signed up, until you cancel. You'll find your renewal date in Manage my subscription and on your receipt email.

When will my first issue arrive?

We mail each issue toward the end of the month. Subscribe by the 10th and your first issue is that month's; after the 10th, it's next month's. Issues usually arrive within one to two weeks of mailing.

What's in each issue?

Four true car stories written by readers, about the roads they traveled, the families they loved and the faith that saw them through. Each issue also has a note from Tommy, a Do You Remember? page, Name That Car, a large-print word search or crossword, and Letters to Tommy from other readers. Everything is printed in large type, with no outside advertising.

Every envelope also holds a surprise from Tommy: something small and real you can hold, handpicked by Tommy and different every month, like a card from a car trivia board game.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage my subscription and sign in with the email you used at checkout. You can also email us, or mail us a note with your name and mailing address, and we'll cancel it for you. Gift subscriptions and subscriptions paid by check don't renew, so there's nothing to cancel.

When do I need to cancel so I'm not charged again?

Before your next billing date. Monthly plans renew on the same day each month as the day you signed up. Yearly plans renew on the same date each year. You'll find your next billing date in Manage my subscription and on your receipt emails.

  • Online or by email: cancel by the day before your billing date.
  • By mail: your note counts on the day it reaches us, so mail it at least a week before your billing date.

For example, if you signed up on March 20, cancel by April 19 and you won't be charged on April 20. If a charge ever goes through after you've asked us to cancel, we'll refund it in full.

Do you offer refunds?

No. Payments aren't refundable, including for part of a month or year. Canceling stops future charges, and you'll keep receiving the issues you've already paid for until your month or year is up. Gift subscriptions and check orders are paid once and aren't refundable either. If an issue doesn't arrive, email us and we'll mail a replacement.

How do I change my mailing address?

If you pay by card, update it under Manage my subscription. If you paid by check, received Tommy Stories as a gift, or someone else pays for your subscription, email us at support@tommystories.com or mail us your old and new address. Changes made by the 10th apply to that month's issue.

Can I pay by check?

Yes. Mail a check for 6 months ($99.99), 1 year ($199.99) or 2 years ($329.99), made out to Tommy Stories LLC, with our printable order form or a note with your name and mailing address, to:

Tommy Stories
3151 Cape Horn Rd, Unit #2079

Can I give it as a gift, or send it to someone else?

Yes, two ways. Subscribe monthly or yearly and enter their name and address for shipping at checkout. It keeps coming to them every month, billed to you, until you cancel. Or give a gift subscription of 6 months, 1 year or 2 years, paid once, which doesn't renew on its own.

Can I tell a friend about Tommy Stories?

Please do. On our Share with a friend page, give us up to 5 friends' email addresses and add a note if you like. We'll email each of them an invitation from you, with a friend offer: $12.99 a month for their first 3 months on the Monthly plan. If they haven't subscribed after a week, we send one short reminder, and that's all.

My issue didn't arrive. What should I do?

Email us at support@tommystories.com with your name and mailing address, and we'll mail a replacement.

Do you mail outside the United States?

Not yet. We mail to U.S. addresses only for now.

How do I share my own story?

Send it through our story form, by email to support@tommystories.com, or by mail. We look for true stories about life, family or faith with a car somewhere in them, about 600 to 1,200 words long. You can include up to 2 photos, which we print in black and white. Please mail copies, not originals, since we can't return mail.
