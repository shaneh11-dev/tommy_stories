Share your story

Every issue of Tommy Stories is written by readers. Send us a true story about your life, your family or your faith. It just needs a car somewhere in it, and it could appear in a future issue.

  • True stories only. The heart of the story can be your life, your family or your faith. It just needs a car somewhere in it, even a small part.
  • About 600 to 1,200 words. That's roughly 2 to 5 typed pages, double-spaced.
  • Type it in the box below, attach a file, or mail it to us. Handwritten is fine.
  • We'll only fix spelling, grammar and typos, and we'll check with you before your story is printed.

*Because we receive so many stories, we can't publish every one, and a story may run several months after it's sent.
