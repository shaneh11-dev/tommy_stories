Share your story

Every issue of Tommy Stories is written by readers. Send us a true story about your life, your family or your faith. It just needs a car somewhere in it, and it could appear in a future issue.

  • True stories only. The heart of the story can be your life, your family or your faith. It just needs a car somewhere in it, even a small part.
  • About 600 to 1,200 words. That's roughly 2 to 5 typed pages, double-spaced.
  • Type it in the box below, attach a file, email it to support@tommystories.com, or mail it to us. Handwritten is fine.
  • Have photos? Send up to 2: the car, the people or the place in your story. Upload them below or put them right in your story file, attach them to your email, or mail copies (not originals; we can't return mail). We print photos in black and white.
  • We'll only fix spelling, grammar and typos, and we'll check with you before your story is printed.

*Because we receive so many stories, we can't publish every one, and a story may run several months after it's sent.

How can we reach you about your story?

Give an email address, a mailing address, or both. We only use them to contact you about your story.

Year, make and model, if you know them. List as many as you like. For example: 1965 Ford Mustang, 1978 Chevy pickup.

Keep it short, just a few words. A good title often names the car, a place, the moment everything changed, or something someone said. For example: “Sandbags in the Back,” “Riding Above the Cab” or “The Camaro Came Home.” Not sure yet? Leave it blank, and we’ll suggest one when we contact you.

Prefer to email or mail it?

Email your story to support@tommystories.com, or mail it to the address below. Typed or handwritten is fine. Either way, include your first name and last initial, your city and state, how you'd like your name to appear, the car(s) in your story, and an email or mailing address so we can reach you.

You can include up to 2 photos: attach them to your email, or mail copies, not originals, since we can't return mail. We print photos in black and white.

By email, add a line saying you agree to our story submission terms. By mail, add a signed note saying the same.
