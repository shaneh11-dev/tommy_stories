# Facebook content plan

The guide for drafting Tommy Stories Facebook posts, whether Claude drafts
the weekly batch or someone writes one by hand. See README.md in this folder
for the file format and how posts are approved and published.

## Who we're talking to

- **Readers:** mostly 60 and up. Car people, church people, grandparents.
  They read on phones and tablets, often with larger text. They remember
  full-service gas stations, drive-ins and the family station wagon.
- **Gift givers:** their grown children and grandchildren, looking for
  something meaningful for Dad, Mom or Grandpa.

## Voice

Warm, plain and friendly, like a neighbor at a cruise night. Short sentences.
No slang, no hype, no ALL CAPS shouting, no hashtag clouds (one or two at
most, or none). One or two emoji at most per post. Faith is part of who we
are: speak about it gently and sincerely, never preachy, never political.
Sign-offs aren't needed; the Page name shows who's talking.

## Weekly rhythm (Eastern time)

Default: 1 post a day at 9:00 a.m., plus an evening post (7:00 p.m.) on
the busiest days. Adjust the count to what the owner chooses.

- **Monday: Do You Remember?** Prices or everyday details from one year
  (`remember` card), with a question ("What did you pay for your first
  car?").
- **Tuesday: question of the week.** One big question that invites a story
  (`question` card): "What was the first car you ever drove?", "Who taught
  you to drive?", "What car did you take your driving test in?"
- **Wednesday: story spotlight.** A few lines from a printed story (`quote`
  card), with the writer's permission (the story agreement covers it), or a
  call for stories ("Got a car story? We want to print it.").
- **Thursday: Name That Car.** Three clues worth 3, 2 and 1 points
  (`name-that-car` card). Answer Friday morning (`answer` card).
- **Friday: the answer**, plus how many points people scored.
- **Saturday: memory lane.** A fun car fact or a "this week in car history"
  item, or a photo the owner took.
- **Sunday: a gentle word.** A short, warm thought for the week, or a
  thank-you to readers. Text-only posts work well here.

Around holidays, lean into the season: gift subscriptions in November and
December (link: tommystories.com/gift), Mother's and Father's Day, the
Fourth of July road trip.

## Rules

- **Every fact must be right.** Check each year, price, name and number
  against a reliable source (encyclopedias, the automaker, government
  data). List the sources in the pull request for the batch. If a fact
  can't be confirmed, leave it out.
- **Don't spoil an issue.** Never use a Name That Car car, a Do You
  Remember? topic or a story from an issue that hasn't mailed yet.
- **Real people.** Quote printed stories only, using the writer's chosen
  byline. Don't name or picture private people otherwise. Never post a
  subscriber's details.
- **Pictures.** Use the card templates, photos the owner took, or reader
  photos sent with permission. Never copy photos from the internet, car
  makers' ads or other Pages.
- **Links.** At most one link per post, in the text. Use tommystories.com
  pages: /join, /gift, /share, /friend, /write. Many posts need no link.
- **Claims.** Describe Tommy Stories accurately: four true reader stories
  each month in a large-print booklet, plus the extras and a small surprise
  in every envelope; $16.99 a month or $199.99 a year; gifts for 6 months,
  1 year or 2 years. No invented testimonials, numbers of readers or
  endorsements.
- **Steer clear** of politics, news controversies, health advice and
  anything that would embarrass a reader in front of their pastor.

## A good batch

- A week of posts, Monday to Sunday, in `social/posts/` with their cards
  in `social/cards/` and pictures in `social/posts/images/`.
- Times spread at least a few hours apart; never two at the same time.
- The pull request lists each post's day, time and kind, plus the
  sources for every fact.
