It was the Chevrolet Corvair! 🎉

Built from 1960 to 1969, the Corvair was Chevy's answer to the little Volkswagen Beetle, with an air-cooled six-cylinder engine in the back. Ralph Nader's book made it famous for the wrong reasons, but a 1972 government study found the early Corvair was no harder to control than other cars of its day.
