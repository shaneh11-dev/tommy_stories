Last updated October 25, 2026

Your subscription

A Tommy Stories subscription costs $16.99 a month, including shipping to U.S. addresses. When you subscribe, you authorize us to charge your payment method $16.99 each month, on the date you signed up, until you cancel. Payments are processed by Stripe.

Price changes

If the price changes, we'll tell you by email or mail at least 30 days before your first charge at the new price. You can cancel before then.

Canceling

You can cancel anytime under Manage my subscription, by emailing support@tommystories.com, or by calling [PHONE]. Your subscription stays active until the end of the month you've paid for, and you'll receive any issue you've already paid for.

Refunds

If you cancel before your first issue is mailed, we'll refund your first payment in full. After an issue has been mailed, the payment for that month isn't refundable. If an issue doesn't arrive, contact us and we'll mail a replacement.

Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions are paid once, up front, for 3, 6 or 12 months. They don't renew automatically. If a gift can't be delivered, contact us and we'll help fix the address or refund the issues not yet mailed.

Delivery

We mail issues by U.S. Mail around the middle of each month to U.S. addresses only. Subscriptions started by the 10th begin with that month's issue. Delivery times depend on the Postal Service.

Story submissions

  • You wrote it, and it is a true account of your own life or experience.
  • You have the right to share it, and you have permission from, or have changed the names of, people who could be identified in it.

You keep ownership of your story. You give Tommy Stories permission to edit it for length and clarity, and to publish it in our printed issues, on our website, and in future Tommy Stories collections. We'll contact you before your story is first printed. We can't return submissions, and we can't print every story we receive.
