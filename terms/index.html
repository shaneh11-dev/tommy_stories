Terms

Last updated October 25, 2026

Your subscription

A Tommy Stories subscription costs $16.99 a month or $199.99 a year, including shipping to U.S. addresses. When you subscribe, you authorize us to charge your payment method that amount each month or each year, on the date you signed up, until you cancel. We'll email you a reminder about 30 days before a yearly plan renews. Payments are processed by Stripe.

Price changes

If the price changes, we'll tell you by email or mail at least 30 days before your first charge at the new price. You can cancel before then.

Canceling

You can cancel anytime under Manage my subscription, by emailing support@tommystories.com, or by mailing us a note with your name and mailing address. To avoid your next charge, cancel online or by email by the day before your next billing date. A mailed note takes effect on the day we receive it. Canceling stops future charges, and the issues you've already paid for keep coming until the end of the month or year you've paid for.

Refunds

Payments aren't refundable, including for partial months or years, gift subscriptions and check orders. Canceling stops future charges, and you'll keep receiving the issues you've already paid for. If we charge you after you've asked us to cancel, we'll refund that charge. If an issue doesn't arrive, contact us and we'll mail a replacement.

Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions and subscriptions paid by check are paid once, up front, for 6 months, 1 year or 2 years. They don't renew automatically. If a gift can't be delivered, contact us and we'll help fix the address.

Delivery

We mail issues by U.S. Mail around the middle of each month to U.S. addresses only. Subscriptions started by the 10th begin with that month's issue. Delivery times depend on the Postal Service.

Story submissions

When you send us a story, you confirm that:

  • You wrote it, and it is a true account of your own life or experience.
  • You have the right to share it, and you have permission from, or have changed the names of, people who could be identified in it.

You keep ownership of your story. You give Tommy Stories permission to fix spelling, grammar and typos, and to publish it in our printed issues, on our website, and in future Tommy Stories collections. We won't otherwise change your words, and we'll contact you before your story is first printed. Your name appears the way you choose on the form: first name and last initial, first name only, or initials, each with your city and state, or no name at all. We can't return submissions, we can't print every story we receive, and a story may run several months after it's sent.
